# Safety and limits

ORBITCARE is an educational prototype, not a medical device. Its comparisons are deterministic and illustrative. A flag indicates a recorded change worth reviewing, not a diagnosis, treatment recommendation, or flight clearance.

The optional reference cohorts are de-identified, small, and have limited timepoints. Values are displayed as recorded with their supplied units and laboratory ranges. Missing history stays missing; the app does not fabricate a baseline.

Synthetic Asteria-7 data is clearly labeled and must never be mixed silently with a real dataset. Radiation is an environmental measure, not an immune-system measurement.
